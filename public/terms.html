Terms of use

Updated 9 September 2026

Tourist is an experimental app for discovering stories about nearby places. These terms cover your use of Tourist. The privacy policy explains how location, questions and research are processed.

Stories and sources

Research is generated with AI and can contain mistakes, omissions or outdated information. Sources are provided so you can check the account. A linked source does not guarantee that every claim is correct. Stories may include documented facts, disputed accounts or folklore; those distinctions matter.

Distances are approximate straight-line distances. A map marker does not establish public access, a walking route or a place's opening hours. Use local signs and current information when visiting.

Google Maps

Tourist uses Google Maps and Places to locate places and display maps. By using these features, you agree to the Google Terms of Service and the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service, which are incorporated into these terms for those features. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Keep Google and other source attributions with their content. Maps, source pages and other third-party material remain subject to their owners' terms.

An evolving prototype

Features and availability may change while Tourist is being developed. Research can take time or fail, and saved information can become unavailable. Do not use Tourist to disrupt the service or attempt to access private systems or credentials.
